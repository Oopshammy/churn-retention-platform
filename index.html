🧠 Production ML Pipeline Architecture & Design Decisions
A comprehensive Machine Learning pipeline tailored for Indian Telecom enterprise deployment. Every phase balances statistical rigor, game-theoretic explainability, and financial ROI in Indian Rupees (₹).
1. Indian Recharge Attribute Modeling
• 7 Plan Features: Directly incorporates ConnectionType, PlanPrice_INR, PlanValidityDays (28, 56, 84, 90, 365 Days), DataAllowance_GB, DataQuotaMode, SMSQuota, and CallsQuota.
• MonthlyEffectiveARPU: Computed as PlanPrice_INR / (PlanValidityDays / 30.0) to accurately measure revenue exposure across varying plan lengths.
• Data Density: Synthesized EffectiveMonthlyData_GB capturing true data generosity (Daily vs Bulk).
2. Intact Account Demographics & Add-ons
• Demographics Intact: Fully preserves Gender, SeniorCitizen, Partner, and Dependents in the model and UI.
• Ecosystem Stickiness: Cross-service density score (0 to 9) measuring subscriber engagement across Security Shield, Cloud Storage, OTT Streaming Bundle, Live TV, and Tech Support add-ons.
3. XGBoost Hyperparameter Tuning & Threshold Optimization
• Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation: Tuned across tree depth, estimators, learning rate, and subsample ratios.
• Class Imbalance Handling: Applied scale_pos_weight = 2.77 in XGBoost.
• Optimal F1 Cutoff (0.64): Elevates precision by +7.4%, substantially reducing wasteful discount giveaways.
4. Explainable AI & Prescriptive ROI Engine
• SHAP TreeExplainer: Deconstructs tree ensemble decisions into exact additive feature contributions in log-odds space.
• Prescriptive Retention Playbook: Top positive SHAP risk factor automatically activates a targeted intervention play.
• Financial ROI Simulator: Calculates intervention cost against gross annual revenue saved to deliver verifiable Net ROI in Indian Rupees (₹).
